The Fig Tree

This moment is like the fig tree analogy from Sylvia Plath's The Bell Jar. In it, the narrator imagines her future as a fig tree, with every branch holding a different fig — a husband, a happy home, a brilliant career, world travel, and more. Unable to choose just one, she watches helplessly as the figs shrivel and fall, one by one, until none are left. It captures the fear that choosing one path means losing every other one — but refusing to choose at all loses them all anyway.

Would you rather choose with your heart, or your brain?
